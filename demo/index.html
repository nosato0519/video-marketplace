FAQ

What does OTT mean?

OTT stands for “over the top,” meaning a content provider delivers video directly to viewers over the internet rather than through broadcast or cable platforms.

What is an OTT platform?

An OTT platform is a streaming service that supplies content online and lets viewers access it directly.

How does OTT work?

OTT platforms deliver video over a high-speed internet connection to connected devices.

Can I get started today?

Yes. Create your channel, add content, choose a monetization model, and launch.
