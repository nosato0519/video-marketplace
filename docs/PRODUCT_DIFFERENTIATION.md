# Product differentiation v0.1

## What we are actually selling
The product is not positioned as a theme, landing page, CMS skin or simple video-player script.

It is positioned as a **self-hosted marketplace foundation for operating a multi-seller video business**.

## The easy-to-understand comparison

### Simple video-selling template
- Create a product page
- Add a video
- Add a price
- Sell

### This product's intended scope
- Buyer storefront
- Seller onboarding
- Seller product management
- Resumable media workflow
- Content review/moderation
- Product publication controls
- Buyer purchase/library flow
- Orders and payment events
- Seller earnings and settlement
- Payout workflow
- Private media delivery
- Language management
- Currency management
- Region controls
- Mobile-friendly no-code admin
- Security/audit foundations
- Installation and operational documentation

The important difference is the **operating loop**, not the number of menu items.

## The strongest demonstration
The sales demo should show one video moving through the complete lifecycle:

1. Admin enables a seller.
2. Seller creates a video product.
3. Seller uploads/resumes the media.
4. Product enters review.
5. Admin reviews and approves it.
6. Product appears in the storefront.
7. Buyer opens the product page.
8. Buyer purchases in sandbox.
9. Purchase appears in the buyer library.
10. Authorized delivery starts.
11. Seller sees the sale and calculated earnings.
12. Admin sees the order and settlement state.
13. Seller requests payout.
14. Admin reviews the payout request.

This sequence communicates the platform's value faster than a long feature list.

## Product promise
The product should make a buyer of the software think:

**「自分で動画マーケットプレイスを作るなら、これを土台にした方がゼロから全部作るより圧倒的に早い。」**

That statement is a positioning goal, not a guaranteed time-saving claim. Actual savings depend on the purchaser's requirements and technical environment.

## Sales assets to create before launch
- Live or locally reproducible demo
- Short product walkthrough video
- Buyer storefront screenshots
- Seller dashboard screenshots
- Mobile admin screenshots
- Complete marketplace lifecycle demonstration
- Installation walkthrough
- Architecture overview for technical buyers

- Security/deployment checklist
- FAQ
- License/support/update policy

## Do not oversell
The product should look powerful because it is genuinely useful, not because the sales page hides limitations. Clearly label unfinished, optional, third-party and deployment-specific functionality.
