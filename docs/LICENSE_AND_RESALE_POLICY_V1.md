# License & Resale Policy V1

## Purpose
Define a commercial license that makes the distribution product understandable and reduces accidental unauthorized redistribution.

## License must explicitly cover
- number/type of installations permitted;
- modification rights;
- production/commercial operation rights;
- whether client projects may be built from the software;
- whether source code may be shared;
- whether the original ZIP/package may be redistributed;
- whether sublicensing is permitted;
- support/update entitlement;
- attribution requirements where applicable;
- termination for material breach.

## Default commercial protection principle
The sales package should not grant an implied right to resell or redistribute the original marketplace package merely because the buyer receives source code.

## Product content vs software
The software license does not automatically grant rights to third-party videos, images, fonts, trademarks, payment-provider assets or other included materials. Demo assets must have appropriate rights and their licenses must be documented.

## Anti-resale design
Where technically appropriate, release metadata can identify the purchased software version/license without embedding personal secrets in the application. License validation must not create a single point of failure for legitimate offline development or documented recovery workflows.

## Sales-page wording rule
Marketing copy must describe the actual license. Avoid absolute claims such as "cannot be resold" unless the legal license and technical controls support the exact claim.

## Legal review
The final license is a legal document. Before commercial sale, it should be reviewed for the target sales jurisdictions and business model by an appropriately qualified professional.
