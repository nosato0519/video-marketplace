# Adult-content deployment requirements

This document describes product requirements for operators who choose to deploy the general-purpose marketplace for lawful adult video content.

## Positioning
Adult support is a deployment capability, not the identity of the core platform. The same buyer, seller and operator workflows should remain usable for permitted adult content.

## Before activation
The operator must independently verify the laws, regulations, taxes, privacy obligations, age requirements, record-keeping obligations, content restrictions and payment-provider policies applicable to every intended jurisdiction.

## Required controls
- Adult-category/content flagging
- Age/eligibility gate where required
- Seller identity/verification workflow where required
- Rights and consent declarations
- Content moderation queue
- Reporting and takedown workflow
- Prohibited-content policy enforcement
- Country/region restrictions
- Payment-provider/category compatibility controls
- Private media storage and authorized delivery
- Audit logs for sensitive moderation/admin actions
- Configurable terms, privacy, content and refund policies

## Provider compatibility
Payment and payout providers must be treated as configurable adapters. The application must not claim that a provider supports adult content unless the operator has verified the provider's current terms for the actual business model and jurisdictions.

## UX requirement
Adult-content users should receive the same clean, professional product experience as other supported categories, while required eligibility, policy and warning controls remain clear and unavoidable where applicable.

## Sales copy rule
Never advertise adult deployment as universally permitted. Use language such as:

> **Suitable for adult-video deployments where legally permitted and supported by the operator's chosen payment, storage and service providers.**

Do not promise:
- universal legal compliance;
- universal payment support;
- anonymous operation where identity verification is required;
- guaranteed acceptance by any provider;
- guaranteed availability in every country.
