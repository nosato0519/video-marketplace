# Content verticals and broad commercial use

## Product philosophy
The platform is intentionally general-purpose. It should not be architected around one content category.

The same core marketplace can support different video businesses through categories, policies, moderation rules, regional availability and provider configuration.

## Example verticals
- General entertainment/video creators
- Educational and instructional videos
- Fitness and training
- Tutorials and how-to content
- Independent filmmakers
- Music/performance video where rights permit
- Events and recorded performances
- Professional training libraries sold per product
- Niche creator communities
- Adult video content, where the operator has confirmed legal eligibility, consent/rights, age requirements, moderation requirements, regional restrictions and payment-provider compatibility

## Why this matters commercially
A general-purpose product can be marketed to a wider customer base than an adult-only script. Adult support can remain an optional deployment use case rather than the identity of the core software.

This lets a prospective buyer understand:

**"I can use the same platform for my own video business, even if my niche changes."**

## Adult-content readiness
The architecture should make adult-content deployment possible without redesigning the entire marketplace, while keeping adult-specific controls configurable and explicit.

Required areas include:
- age/eligibility controls;
- seller identity/verification where required;
- rights and consent declarations;
- content moderation and reporting;
- prohibited-content rules;
- country/region restrictions;
- payment-provider compatibility;
- privacy and secure media delivery;
- auditability;
- appropriate policy/terms pages.

The product must never imply that adult content is accepted by every payment provider or lawful in every jurisdiction.

## Marketing rule
Do not make the sales page adult-only unless a particular release is intentionally packaged that way. The stronger commercial position is:

**A professional video marketplace platform that can be adapted to many video businesses — including adult deployments where permitted.**
