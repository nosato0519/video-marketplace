# Demo standard

The sales demo is part of the product, not an afterthought. It must make the marketplace understandable and credible within a few minutes.

## Demo flow

1. Landing page: explain the product and value proposition immediately.
2. Marketplace: browse realistic products with polished cards and filters.
3. Product detail: show preview, description, seller information, trust signals, price, and purchase CTA.
4. Buyer account: show library and purchased content access.
5. Seller dashboard: show product creation, publishing state, sales, and earnings.
6. Admin dashboard: show moderation, reports, transactions, and audit history.
7. Settings: demonstrate domain, branding, email, payment, storage, and localization configuration.

## Demo requirements

- Use realistic sample data, not empty placeholder screens.
- Keep the demo safe and self-contained.
- Provide a guided path with obvious next actions.
- Make the product's strongest differentiators visible without requiring a technical explanation.
- Ensure every major screen is responsive and visually polished.
