# Seller / Creator Guide

## Seller onboarding

1. Create a seller account.
2. Verify the email address.
3. Complete the required seller verification level.
4. Provide payout information through the supported payout flow.
5. Read and accept the seller agreement and content rules.
6. Complete the seller profile.

## Publishing a video

1. Create a new product.
2. Upload the required video and thumbnail assets through the supported private upload flow.
3. Enter title, description, category and tags.
4. Set price and permitted currencies/display rules.
5. Select streaming/download options allowed by the operator.
6. Provide any required rights, consent or identity documentation.
7. Submit the listing for moderation.
8. Wait for approval before assuming the listing is publicly available.

## Seller responsibilities

The seller must have the necessary rights and permissions for every asset submitted. Do not upload stolen, infringing, non-consensual, illegal or prohibited content.

Adult content, where enabled, has additional requirements. Sellers must comply with the platform's age, identity, consent, rights, recordkeeping, moderation and regional rules. A seller must not assume that content permitted in one country or by one provider is permitted everywhere.

## Sales and payouts

The seller dashboard shows eligible sales, platform fees, refunds/adjustments and available payout balance according to the configured accounting rules.

Payout timing may include a hold period for fraud, refunds, disputes or compliance review.

## Account security

Use a unique password, enable MFA when available, protect payout credentials and never share API keys or private credentials.
