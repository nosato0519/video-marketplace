# Revenue-first roadmap

The project priority is to build a reliable, usable video marketplace before expanding into unrelated projects.

## Product priorities

1. Core marketplace experience
2. Seller onboarding and product publishing
3. Buyer discovery and purchase flow
4. Payments, refunds, and seller settlement integration
5. Video delivery and access control
6. Security, moderation, reporting, and auditability
7. Admin operations and support tooling
8. Analytics and revenue optimization
9. Reliability, monitoring, backups, and deployment

## Principle

Revenue is a goal, not a guarantee. Product decisions should prioritize a trustworthy customer experience, sustainable operating costs, legal/compliance requirements, and measurable paths to revenue.
