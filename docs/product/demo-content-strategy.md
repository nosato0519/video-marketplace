# Demo content strategy

The first product remains a general-purpose video marketplace. The sales demo may include a clearly labeled adult-content example to demonstrate that the platform can support age-restricted categories, while keeping the core product architecture category-agnostic.

## Rules

- Adult content is demo content, not a hard-coded product assumption.
- Category, age-restriction, moderation, and policy settings must be configurable.
- The same platform should support ordinary video categories without requiring code changes.
- A future adult-specialized edition can build on the same core marketplace after the general product is production-ready.
