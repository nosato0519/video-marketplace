# Adult marketplace readiness

Adult-focused positioning is a possible future specialization, but it is not enabled by default in the current build.

## Commercial opportunity

The international digital adult-content market is large, and creator-led paid content is an established business model. However, adult specialization materially increases compliance, payment, moderation, and age-assurance requirements.

## Additional requirements if enabled

- Robust age assurance for users where required
- Creator identity and age verification
- Consent and rights documentation for every participant and upload
- Strong reporting, takedown, and evidence workflows
- Prohibited-content detection and moderation
- Payment-processor eligibility and monitoring
- Region-specific legal and policy configuration
- Privacy-preserving handling of age and identity data
- Clear audit trails and retention/deletion policies

The product should therefore remain configurable so that adult specialization can be evaluated later without contaminating the core marketplace architecture.
