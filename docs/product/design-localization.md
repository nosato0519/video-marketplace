# Design and market localization

The product should have one strong, modern visual system rather than separate Japanese and overseas products.

## Design principles

- Clear hierarchy and generous spacing
- Strong product imagery and video previews
- Simple primary actions
- Responsive mobile-first layouts
- Trust signals near purchase and seller information
- Consistent typography, buttons, forms, cards, and navigation
- Accessible contrast and keyboard-friendly interactions

## Market adaptation

The core visual language can remain shared while content, currency, date formats, language, trust messaging, and checkout expectations are localized. Do not fork the entire UI by country unless real user data proves a need.

## Sale requirement

The product should support localization without requiring source-code rewrites for each buyer. Branding, locale, currency, and key copy should be configurable where practical.
