# General-purpose video marketplace

The first product is a general-purpose, configurable video marketplace designed for international sale.

## Product goal

Provide a deployable marketplace that can be adapted to different video categories without changing the core application.

## Configuration-first areas

- Categories and subcategories
- Age restrictions
- Seller requirements
- Moderation policies
- Branding
- Locale and currency
- Payment provider
- Email provider
- Storage and video delivery
- Platform fees

The core product remains category-agnostic. Specialized editions can be created later from the same foundation.
