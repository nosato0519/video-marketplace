# Trust & Safety V1

## Goal
Make the marketplace feel trustworthy for buyers, sellers and operators while giving the operator practical controls for abuse, disputes and content safety.

## Buyer trust
- Clear seller identity/profile information where appropriate.
- Visible product terms before purchase.
- Clear pricing, currency and refund/access policy.
- Purchase confirmation and order history.
- Easy reporting of problematic content or transactions.
- No misleading download/view availability claims.

## Seller trust
- Clear onboarding requirements.
- Transparent review states and rejection reasons.
- Ability to report unauthorized redistribution.
- Clear payout status and transaction history.
- No silent changes to product price or terms after a confirmed order.

## Operator controls
- Seller/product moderation queue.
- Content takedown and restoration workflow.
- User suspension with audit trail.
- Order/refund review workflow.
- Security incident workflow.
- Abuse reports with status tracking.
- Role-based access to sensitive moderation data.

## Content safety
The platform must support category-specific rules and configurable moderation. Adult deployments require additional age/eligibility, rights/consent, moderation and legal-policy controls appropriate to the operator's jurisdiction and payment/hosting providers.

## Dispute handling
A dispute should have:
- a unique case identifier;
- affected order/product/account references;
- status and timestamps;
- assigned reviewer where applicable;
- evidence references without unnecessary personal data;
- outcome and reason;
- audit history.

## Privacy principle
Collect the minimum information required for account, commerce, security and moderation functions. Avoid exposing private buyer information to sellers unless necessary and permitted.

## Trust UX
Safety controls should be understandable. Avoid unexplained warnings or frightening language. Explain why an action is blocked and what the legitimate user can do next.

## Release requirement
Trust and safety is part of the core product, not an optional add-on. Critical buyer, seller and moderation flows must be included in end-to-end testing before release.
