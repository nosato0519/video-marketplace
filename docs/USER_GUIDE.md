# Buyer / User Guide

## Getting started

1. Select your preferred language.
2. Create an account or sign in.
3. Browse categories or search for a video.
4. Open the product page to review the description, preview, price and available delivery options.
5. Complete checkout using an available payment method.
6. Open your purchased library.
7. Stream or download the product according to its stated policy.

## Purchases

Keep your account secure. Never share your password or authentication codes.

A purchase does not automatically mean unrestricted redistribution. The buyer must follow the product license, site terms and applicable law.

## Downloads

Availability, expiry and download limits depend on the product settings. A product may be streaming-only.

## Reports

Use the report function when you believe a listing violates the site's rules, copyright requirements, privacy rights, consent requirements or applicable law. Do not use reports as a substitute for emergency services or law enforcement.

## Account safety

Use a unique password, enable available multi-factor authentication, sign out from shared devices and contact the operator through the official support channel if you suspect account compromise.
