# Next-site idea evaluation v0.1

## Goal
Choose the next product only after the first marketplace is stable. Prioritize products that reuse the marketplace foundation while opening a distinct customer and revenue opportunity.

## Candidate A — Digital asset marketplace
Sell downloadable digital products such as video, audio, templates, graphics, documents and creator packs.

**Why it is attractive:** maximum reuse of catalog, sellers, orders, payments, payouts, moderation, storage, localization and admin.

**Differentiation needed:** asset-specific previews, file bundles, license selection, versioning and download entitlements.

**Commercial potential:** broad.

## Candidate B — Creator membership/community platform
Creators sell individual content plus optional memberships, posts, private communities and supporter benefits.

**Why it is attractive:** natural extension of seller profiles, buyer accounts, content library and payments.

**Challenge:** memberships/community moderation create substantially more product complexity.

**Commercial potential:** high, but more operationally complex.

## Candidate C — Online course marketplace
Creators sell courses made from video, documents, quizzes and progress tracking.

**Why it is attractive:** strong use of video marketplace foundations and clear business value.

**Differentiation needed:** lessons, course structure, progress, certificates, instructor dashboard.

**Commercial potential:** broad and relatively brand-safe.

## Candidate D — Stock/media marketplace
Creators sell licensed video clips, photos, music and other media.

**Why it is attractive:** strong marketplace fit and international audience.

**Differentiation needed:** advanced metadata, licensing, search, preview/watermarking and asset formats.

**Commercial potential:** high, but search and licensing quality are critical.

## Candidate E — Appointment/service marketplace
Users discover providers, book services and pay online.

**Why it is attractive:** large market and familiar marketplace model.

**Challenge:** this is less reusable than a digital-content product and introduces schedules, cancellations, availability and potentially location-sensitive operations.

**Commercial potential:** high, but higher divergence from the first product.

## Current recommendation
**First candidate to investigate: Digital asset marketplace.**

Reason: it can reuse the largest portion of the first site's infrastructure while expanding beyond video. The second candidate should then be evaluated using real customer feedback and sales results from the first product.

## Decision gate
Do not commit to the next product yet. Before development starts, compare:
- actual first-product sales;
- customer requests;
- support burden;
- installation friction;
- recurring feature requests;
- payment/provider constraints;
- legal/compliance complexity;
- development reuse percentage;
- expected selling price;
- expected support cost;
- competition and differentiation.
