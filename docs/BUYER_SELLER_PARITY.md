# Buyer and seller parity requirements v0.1

The marketplace has two equally important customers: the person buying a video and the person using the platform to sell one. Neither experience should be treated as secondary.

## Buyer must be able to
- Discover products quickly.
- Understand exactly what is being sold.
- Check seller identity/profile and product information.
- See price, currency, language, subtitles and delivery method before purchase.
- Complete checkout without unnecessary steps.
- Recover from payment interruption.
- Access purchased content immediately after verified payment.
- Find purchases again later.
- Contact support or report an issue without hunting through the site.

## Seller must be able to
- Register and understand what is required before selling.
- Create and save a product without completing everything in one session.
- Upload large video files reliably.
- Understand processing status.
- See exactly why a product needs changes or was rejected.
- Understand platform fees and estimated proceeds before publication.
- Publish approved content without technical work.
- See sales and payout status accurately.
- Manage products from mobile and desktop.
- Resolve common problems without contacting support for every minor issue.

## Shared design rules
- Consistent navigation and terminology.
- Mobile-first responsive behavior.
- Accessible controls and clear focus states.
- Plain-language errors with a recovery action.
- No destructive action without an understandable confirmation.
- Preserve work whenever possible.
- Do not hide important fees, restrictions or requirements.
- Do not use deceptive conversion tactics.
- Keep buyer-facing and seller-facing workflows visually related so the platform feels like one coherent product.

## Business rule
A successful marketplace needs both sides. Improving buyer conversion without making selling easy can reduce supply; improving seller onboarding without making buying trustworthy can reduce demand. Product development should therefore measure and improve both sides together.
