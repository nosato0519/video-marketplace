# Commercial positioning and sales copy

This document is the source of truth for future product-page, marketplace-listing and ZIP-release copy. Claims must remain accurate to the actual release.

## Positioning
### Short
**Build your own video marketplace — without building the marketplace from scratch.**

A multilingual, multi-currency video marketplace designed for creators, digital publishers and operators who want to sell video content under their own brand.

### One-line value proposition
**A ready-to-customize video marketplace with buyer, seller and no-code admin experiences built into one platform.**

## Product page hero
**Launch a professional video marketplace built for selling, not just showcasing.**

Give creators a place to sell videos, give customers a simple way to discover and purchase content, and manage the marketplace from one responsive admin dashboard.

### Supporting bullets
- Sell individual videos and configurable products.
- Support streaming and controlled downloads.
- Start with English and Japanese, with an architecture ready for additional languages.
- Support multiple currencies and configurable regions.
- Give sellers their own upload, sales and payout workspace.
- Manage routine operations without programming knowledge.
- Manage the marketplace from desktop or smartphone.
- Keep payment, storage and video-processing integrations replaceable where practical.

## Who it is for
- Video creators and publishers
- Digital-content businesses
- Niche video marketplaces
- Agencies building client-owned video platforms
- Operators who want a self-hosted alternative to a hosted marketplace

## Why it is different
**One system for the whole marketplace.** Buyer experience, seller tools and operator controls are designed together rather than bolted on as separate plugins.

**Built for international use.** Localization, currency display and region rules are part of the architecture rather than an afterthought.

**Designed for non-technical operators.** Routine management uses guided screens, validation and clear explanations instead of requiring source-code changes.

**Designed with distribution in mind.** The commercial package includes installation, configuration, security, backup, update and troubleshooting documentation.

## Trust wording
Use precise claims such as:
- “Designed with security controls including authentication, authorization, private media access and auditability.”
- “Payment details are handled by supported payment providers rather than stored as raw card data by the marketplace.”

Do not use unsupported claims such as:
- “100% secure”
- “unhackable”
- “guaranteed sales”
- “guaranteed approval by every payment provider”
- “works in every country”

## Adult-content positioning
The core product is general-purpose. Adult-content operation is an optional use case subject to applicable law, provider policy, moderation requirements, age/consent requirements and regional restrictions. Marketing must never imply that every payment provider or jurisdiction permits adult content.

## ZIP product listing draft
### Title
**Video Marketplace — Self-Hosted Multilingual Video Selling Platform**

### Description
Build and operate your own video marketplace with a buyer storefront, seller workspace and operator dashboard in one self-hosted package.

The platform is designed for businesses and creators who want more control over their storefront, catalog, seller relationships and content delivery than a simple video-player template provides.

The package is designed from the beginning for international deployment, with multilingual UI architecture, multiple currencies, configurable regions and replaceable integrations for payments, storage and video processing.

### What is included
- Buyer storefront and catalog foundation
- Product detail and purchase-flow foundation
- Seller dashboard foundation
- No-code administrator dashboard foundation
- Multilingual UI architecture
- Multi-currency architecture
- Seller workflow and payout foundations
- Moderation and reporting foundations
- Private media delivery architecture
- Database migrations and documentation
- Installation/setup documentation
- Security and deployment guidance
- Backup/recovery and upgrade guidance
- Release acceptance checklist

### Important release note
Feature availability, integrations and deployment requirements vary by release. The final listing must be generated from the release's actual acceptance checklist and must not promise functionality that has not passed testing.

## Pricing philosophy
Do not compete only on low price. Position the product around saved development time, ownership, customization, international readiness and the breadth of included operational tooling.

A final price should be chosen after a production-like release candidate has been tested and the true installation/support burden is known.
