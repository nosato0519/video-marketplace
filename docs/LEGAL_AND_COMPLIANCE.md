# Legal, Compliance and Operational Cautions

## Important

This document is a technical launch checklist, not legal advice. The operator must obtain qualified legal/tax/privacy advice for the actual jurisdictions, business model, content categories and payment providers used.

## Before launch

Confirm, for every intended market:

- Business registration and licensing requirements
- Consumer protection and refund rules
- Privacy/data-protection requirements
- Cookie/tracking requirements
- Tax/VAT/GST obligations
- Digital-content sales requirements
- Copyright and notice/takedown procedures
- Age restrictions and age-verification requirements where applicable
- Rules governing adult content, if enabled
- Consent and performer/participant documentation requirements where applicable
- Recordkeeping and retention requirements
- Payment-provider terms
- Payout-provider terms
- Sanctions/export/geographic restrictions where applicable

## Adult content

Adult support is optional and must be explicitly enabled only after the operator confirms the relevant legal, payment and operational requirements. Never permit prohibited content. The operator must establish procedures for age/identity verification, consent/rights verification, complaints, takedowns, records and region restrictions as required by applicable law and provider rules.

## Privacy

Collect only data necessary for the service. Document the purpose and retention of personal data. Provide appropriate privacy notices and user rights mechanisms for applicable jurisdictions.

## Intellectual property

The operator must establish ownership/licensing rules, seller warranties, complaint handling and a documented notice/takedown process. The platform should not imply that uploading content grants the seller rights they do not have.

## Release requirement

Do not advertise the software as legally compliant worldwide merely because it supports country settings. Technical configurability is not a legal determination.
