# Second Product Plan — Digital Content Marketplace

## Working concept
A self-hosted, international marketplace for selling downloadable and streamable digital content under the operator's own brand.

## Core reuse
Reuse the first product's proven foundations where appropriate:
- buyer accounts and library
- seller onboarding and dashboard
- admin/no-code management
- products/catalog/search
- orders and payment adapter boundary
- fees/revenue share
- earnings and payouts
- moderation/reporting
- localization
- currency/region rules
- private media/file delivery
- audit/security foundations
- installation and distribution tooling

## Product-specific expansion
Support product types such as:
- video
- audio
- photo/image packs
- PDF/document
- templates
- design assets
- downloadable archives
- course materials

Each product type should define its own preview, delivery, metadata, licensing and access rules.

## Commercial goal
Create a second sellable ZIP product that has a wider addressable market than the first video-only product while preserving the strengths learned from the first deployment.

## Release rule
Do not build the second product as a parallel distraction. The first product must first reach a tested release candidate, be manually evaluated by the operator and have reusable modules identified. Then branch the next product from the stable foundation.

## Planned sales positioning
**Sell digital content from your own marketplace — without starting from zero.**

The final sales page must demonstrate the complete buyer → seller → operator lifecycle and clearly distinguish reusable core capabilities from optional third-party integrations.
